VaultZero Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

VaultZero is an offline password manager and encrypted vault. The developer cannot access, collect, or sell your data — the app has no network capability and no accounts.

What is stored on your device

  • Encrypted vault records (AES-256-GCM), wrapped key material, KDF salts and public KDF parameters.
  • Security settings you choose (appearance, timeouts, biometric opt-in).
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash telemetry, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or usage history.

What leaves your device

Only what you explicitly export: an encrypted backup file written through Android's system file picker to a location you choose. It contains no plaintext titles or secrets and is never sent to the developer.

Network

VaultZero declares no INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permission and contains no networking code. It cannot contact any server.

Biometrics

When enabled, Android's biometric prompt unlocks a hardware-backed Keystore key that wraps your vault key. VaultZero never receives fingerprint or face data; matching happens inside the device's secure hardware.

Data deletion

Uninstalling VaultZero deletes its local app data (vault, settings and Keystore wrapping keys). Backups you exported remain wherever you saved them. There is no server-side data to delete.
