# 🛡️ VaultZero `v1.7.6` — the key comes alive

**Release date:** 21 November 2026
**Package:** `com.cipherwell.vaultzero` · **versionCode:** 15
**Min SDK:** 24 · **Target/compile SDK:** 36 · **License:** MIT

Same idea as 1.7.5 — the bow is a **V**, the bit is a **Z** — now treated like a product logo instead
of a pictogram.

## What changed

- **Flat two-tone depth:** the white key sits on a flat `#0B2F7A` offset shadow (no gradients, no
  blur), which gives it weight at large sizes and disappears cleanly at 48 dp.
- **Accent colour:** the V opening is filled cyan (`#6FE3FF`), so the initial reads immediately.
- **Sparkles:** a small glint on the bow edge and a cyan spark, both placed **inside the 66 dp safe
  circle** — the first cut had them clipped by circular launcher masks.
- **Proportions rebalanced:** smaller bow, longer shaft, larger Z bit; the glyph is scaled 0.92 into
  the safe zone so nothing touches a mask edge.
- Adaptive layers, separate monochrome layer (V stays a real hole for themed icons), legacy
  squircle/round PNGs, 512 px icon and the 1024 × 500 feature graphic regenerated from one source.

## Verification

- **131 JVM/Robolectric tests / 0 failures**, lint 0 errors (no functional changes).
- Signed 1.7.6 installed on the API 34 emulator; rendered under the circular launcher mask with the
  sparkle fully visible.
- Monochrome layer checked as a silhouette; store and legacy assets rendered from the same geometry.
