# 🛡️ VaultZero `v1.7.19` — cleaner filter row

**Release date:** 22 November 2026
**Package:** `com.cipherwell.vaultzero` · **versionCode:** 28
**Min SDK:** 24 · **Target/compile SDK:** 36 · **License:** MIT

The filter row is back to a single swipeable line, with a clearer cue and the right surfaces.

## What changed

- **One horizontal scrolling row** for the filters again (no wrapping).
- **Subtle chevron affordance** in the right-hand gutter: a small circular button that signals more
  filters and scrolls the row when tapped. It fades out automatically at the last filter.
- **Chip backgrounds match the Settings segmented buttons** — filled surface-container pills with a
  tinted primary container and primary label when selected, so they separate cleanly from the page in
  both light and dark themes.
- The partially hidden next chip plus the chevron make the sideways swipe obvious without adding
  clutter.

## Verification

- Captured light + dark, English + Spanish on the API 34 emulator with the seeded vault: chevron
