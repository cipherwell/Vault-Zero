# 🛡️ VaultZero `v1.7.22` — stronger haptics

**Release date:** 22 November 2026
**Package:** `com.cipherwell.vaultzero` · **versionCode:** 31
**Min SDK:** 24 · **Target/compile SDK:** 36 · **License:** MIT

Feedback from v1.7.21 was that the vibration was too soft. This release raises it without turning
slider drags into a buzz.

## Changed

- **Toggle / chip / navigation ticks:** 10 ms at 39% amplitude (was 7 ms at 14%).
- **Unlock success pulse:** 18 ms at 67% (was 12 ms at 22%).
- **Generator sliders:** dedicated 7 ms, 27% tick — stronger than before, but controlled across long
  drags.

The on/off switch is unchanged: **Settings → Subtle haptics**, on by default.

## Verification

- **139 JVM/Robolectric tests / 0 failures**, lint 0.
