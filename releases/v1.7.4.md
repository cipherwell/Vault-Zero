# 🛡️ VaultZero `v1.7.4` — icon rollback

**Release date:** 21 November 2026
**Package:** `com.cipherwell.vaultzero` · **versionCode:** 13
**Min SDK:** 24 · **Target/compile SDK:** 36 · **License:** MIT

Reverts the launcher icon to the **v1.7.2 zero-shaped keyhole**. The 1.7.3 fingerprint/zero mark
read as a spiral or target at launcher size, particularly under circular masks, so it is withdrawn
while a better fingerprint direction is designed.

- Icon assets are byte-identical to v1.7.2.
- No functional or cryptographic changes.
