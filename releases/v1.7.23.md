# 🛡️ VaultZero `v1.7.23` — generator history removed

**Release date:** 22 November 2026
**Package:** `com.cipherwell.vaultzero` · **versionCode:** 32
**Min SDK:** 24 · **Target/compile SDK:** 36 · **License:** MIT

Session history wasn’t pulling its weight, and its Spanish header was squeezing the “Borrar” button
into a vertical stack of letters. It’s gone entirely.

## Removed

- The generator’s **session history** card, its in-memory store, timestamps and copy actions.
- The history strings/plurals and the dedicated store tests.
- The generator now keeps **only the current credential**; nothing is retained unless you explicitly
  tap **Save to Vault**.
- Guide (EN/ES) updated to describe the simpler behaviour.

## Verification

- Spanish generator checked after generating several credentials: no history section, no wrapped
  “Borrar”, clean spacing under the settings card.
- **133 JVM/Robolectric tests / 0 failures**, lint 0.
- Shipped APK: `versionCode 32`, `flags=0x0`, `screencap` 0 bytes.
