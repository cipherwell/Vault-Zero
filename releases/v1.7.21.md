# 🛡️ VaultZero `v1.7.21` — subtle haptics

**Release date:** 22 November 2026
**Package:** `com.cipherwell.vaultzero` · **versionCode:** 30
**Min SDK:** 24 · **Target/compile SDK:** 36 · **License:** MIT

Gentle tactile feedback across the app, on by default and fully optional.

## What's new

- **Subtle haptics** on:
  - theme chips, security option chips and Settings switches;
  - vault filter chips, the filter-scroll chevron and bottom navigation;
  - password/passphrase **length sliders** and generator toggles;
  - a slightly fuller pulse on **successful unlock** (password, biometric or setup completion).
- **Settings → Subtle haptics** turns it off instantly; the choice persists across restarts. Default is on.
- Uses a 7 ms tick at ~14% amplitude and a 12 ms success pulse at ~22%, not a strong buzz.
- Adds only the normal `VIBRATE` permission — still no network, location or sensitive permission.

## Verification
